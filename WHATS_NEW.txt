SpotRush - Visual Upgrade v21
=============================

HOW TO CHECK YOU ARE RUNNING THIS BUILD
---------------------------------------
Launch the game. The main menu shows a small "v21" stamp in the
bottom-right corner. If you don't see it, you are running an older
build - delete the old extracted folder and extract this zip fresh
(open project.godot in Godot 4).

WHAT CHANGED IN v21 - THE STORE SHELF + ZERO-FRICTION DUELS
-----------------------------------------------------------
* DUELS WITHOUT TYPING: creating a room now shouts a tiny beacon onto
  your Wi-Fi, and every phone sitting on the JOIN tab lists the game
  under NEARBY GAMES - one tap joins, no reading digits out loud, no
  fat-fingered 192.168.x.x. The game code well stays right below for
  the rare networks that hide devices from each other; both paths run
  through the exact same join flow (watchdog, status line, everything).
* THE GAME CODE PICKS THE RIGHT ADDRESS: phones have several network
  addresses (Wi-Fi, mobile data, VPN tunnels) and the old code showed
  whichever one the OS listed first - sometimes an address your friend
  could never reach. The code is now ranked: home/hotspot Wi-Fi ranges
  first, cellular and VPN ranges last, so the address on screen is the
  address that actually works.
* THE BEACON KNOWS ITS MANNERS: the shout stops the moment your room
  is full (no third phone staring at a game it can't join), resumes if
  the seat frees up, and a phone that is hosting never lists itself on
  its own join tab. Games that go quiet vanish from the list within
  four seconds.
* SCAN THE SQUARE: hosting a duel now stamps a QR square right under
  the game code. Your friend points their phone's own camera app at
  it, copies the code it shows, taps PASTE on the JOIN tab - and the
  game recognises it and knocks on your door in one tap. No camera
  permission, no in-app scanner: the square is drawn by a tiny QR
  generator written from scratch (levels M, versions 1-4), and the
  parser happily digs the code out of whatever chrome the camera app
  wraps around it. The typed game code well stays for the rare Wi-Fi
  that hides devices from each other.
* A NUDGE FOR YOUR STREAK: settings grew a "Daily Streak Reminder"
  switch (off by default, as nudges should be). Turn it on, grant the
  one Android permission, and if you leave the app with your Daily
  Spot unplayed and a live streak, ONE calm notification lands at
  19:00 local - "a quick run keeps your N-day streak alive." Play the
  daily and nothing is ever scheduled; open the app and the pending
  nudge quietly cancels; a streak of zero is never "at risk", so it
  never notifies. The native poster is now IN THE BOX - the prebuilt
  NotificationScheduler Android plugin ships in the project (already
  enabled), the monochrome streak-flame status icon is bundled at
  every density, and building the APK is one icon-copy command
  (android_extras/NOTIFICATIONS.md).
* YOUR RECORDS SURVIVE A NEW PHONE: Android Auto Backup is now
  officially on - settings, best scores, skill rating, the daily
  streak and your rival code all ride the phone's cloud backup and
  come back on reinstall or device migration (android_extras/BACKUP.md
  documents what is covered and how to verify it on a device).
* SELF-TEST GREW AGAIN: a new suite section verifies the beacon codec
  round-trips and rejects junk, that repeat beacons collapse into one
  listing, that expired games disappear and fresh ones revive, and
  that the address ranking puts the working Wi-Fi first. Two more
  sections joined it: one rounds the join-code codec (round-trip,
  camera-app chrome tolerance, junk rejection) and checks the QR
  squares structurally - sizes, finder hearts, timing tracks, dark
  modules, alignment rings - across all four versions; the other
  drives the reminder decision table (schedule today, schedule
  tomorrow, custom hour, on-the-hour edge, played-today cancel,
  disabled cancel, no-streak cancel) so the nudge logic is proven
  without a phone. The generated squares were additionally decoded
  by two independent QR readers, blurred and squashed to half
  resolution, and still scanned. Run it as always:
  godot --headless -s res://scripts/challenges/ChallengeSystemSelfTest.gd
* THE STORE SHELF (behind the curtain): a complete Play Store kit now
  ships in the project's store/ folder - a hostable privacy policy,
  paste-ready store listing copy, Data Safety and IARC rating
  walkthroughs, a step-by-step release checklist with the signing-key
  commands, and a brand-drawn 1024x500 feature graphic. The folder is
  excluded from the exported game bundle; it exists for the publisher,
  not the player. First store version: 1.0.0 (version code 1).

=============================

HOW TO CHECK YOU ARE RUNNING THIS BUILD
---------------------------------------

bottom-right corner. If you don't see it, you are running an older
build - delete the old extracted folder and extract this zip fresh

WHAT CHANGED IN v20 - THE HARDWARE REALITY PASS
-----------------------------------------------
* THE APP SURVIVES ANDROID NOW: pressing Home, taking a call or
  switching apps mid-run used to leave the match running behind your
  back - sounds playing over your music, the round clock draining
  while you were gone. Now the phone telling the app "you are
  backgrounded" freezes a solo run's clock exactly like the quit
  sheet does, and mutes every sound until you come back. Pick up
  where you left off; nothing was stolen from you. Duels stay live
  on purpose (both phones must keep the same beat - your rival is
  not pausing for your phone call), and the v19 "DUEL ENDED" sheet
  still catches the case where the other phone vanished while you
  were away.
* THE SCREEN STAYS AWAKE: SpotRush now tells Android "keep the screen
  on while I am open". DON'T TAP holds and GET READY cards are long
  no-touch moments - the screen used to be free to blank mid-round.
  It won't any more.
* REAL NOTCH & CAMERA HOLE SAFETY: the game now reads the phone's
  actual safe-area insets (punch-hole cameras, status bars, gesture
  pills) and pads every screen's content by exactly the blocked
  amount. On desktop this is a no-op, so editor testing is unchanged;
  on tall 20:9 phones the HUD, footer keys and the version stamp no
  longer risk hiding behind the camera.
* MUSIC, AT LAST: a fully synthesized ambient soundtrack in the same
  soft sound language as the effects - a warm Fmaj7->Am7 pad loop on
  the menu, a quiet A-minor drone bed in the arena, forged on a
  background thread during boot so it never hitches. The game bed is
  deliberately rhythm-free: the round clock's tick owns the rhythm.
* MUSIC AND EFFECTS VOLUME SLIDERS: Settings grew two slider rows.
  Sound effects now run on their own audio bus (separate from music),
  both levels persist and apply instantly, and dragging the effects
  slider gives you a pip so you can hear where you landed.
* AN APP ICON THAT ISN'T A ROBOT: a real SpotRush launcher icon set -
  the lit butter pad with the raised sculpture mark and the coral
  spot-dimple - including the Android adaptive icon layers cut to
  the 66dp safe zone.
* ANDROID EXPORT PRESET SHIPPED: the project now carries a complete
  Android export configuration - portrait lock, immersive mode, the
  INTERNET permission duels need, the VIBRATE permission haptics
  need, arm64+v7a architectures, package name com.spotrush.game,
  version 1.0.0 (code 1) and the launcher icons wired in. Open it in
  the Godot editor with the Android build templates installed and
  export an APK; flip gradle_build/export_format to 1 for an AAB
  when uploading to the Play Console.
* SELF-TEST GREW AGAIN: a new hardware-reality section forges both
  music loops and checks they loop seamlessly at the seam, verifies
  the volume settings round-trip and clamp, drives the pause/resume
  lifecycle and checks safe-area math. Run it as always:
  godot --headless -s res://scripts/challenges/ChallengeSystemSelfTest.gd

SpotRush - Visual Upgrade v19
=============================

HOW TO CHECK YOU ARE RUNNING THIS BUILD
---------------------------------------
Launch the game. The main menu shows a small "v19" stamp in the
bottom-right corner. If you don't see it, you are running an older
build - delete the old extracted folder and extract this zip fresh
(open project.godot in Godot 4).

WHAT CHANGED IN v19 - BACK BUTTON + CONFIRMATION SHEETS
--------------------------------------------------------
* THE BACK BUTTON WORKS NOW: Android's back button used to kill the
  app instantly - mid-duel, mid-run, whenever it was pressed (Godot's
  default quit-on-back). It is now routed through the game instead:
  back closes whatever sheet is open first, walks up a screen when
  there is one, and only at the main menu root asks "LEAVE SPOTRUSH?"
  with EXIT / STAY keys.
* CONFIRMATIONS FOR ANYTHING THAT COSTS PROGRESS: quitting a solo run
  ("QUIT THIS RUN?" - with the round you're on and a reminder the
  points won't be saved), leaving a live duel ("LEAVE THE DUEL?"), and
  closing a room your friend is in ("LEAVE THE ROOM?"). One shared
  neumorphic sheet design everywhere: the coral key is the
  irreversible action, the plain key is the safe one - and the safe
  choice is always one tap of the sheet, one tap outside it, or one
  more press of back away.
* SOLO CLOCK HOLDS ITS BREATH: while a quit question is up in solo
  play, the round timer freezes - deciding should never cost you the
  round. Duels keep running live (both phones must stay on the same
  beat; your rival is not pausing for your dialog).
* NO MORE SILENT DUMPS: if the host leaves a duel mid-match, the
  surviving phone now lands on the menu with a "DUEL ENDED" sheet
  explaining what happened, instead of teleporting in silence.
* DESKTOP PARITY: Escape plays the exact role of hardware back, so
  the whole flow is testable in the Godot editor without a phone.
* CLEAN QUITS UNDER THE HOOD: abandoning a run hard-stops the match
  controller (no timers left ticking, no challenge half-dealt, no
  state leaking into your next match) and drops any duel connection.
  Records only ever see finished runs - a quit run simply never
  reaches the results screen.
* SELF-TEST GREW: a new suite section starts a match, quits it
  mid-countdown and verifies the slate is clean before the next run.
  Run it as always:
  godot --headless -s res://scripts/challenges/ChallengeSystemSelfTest.gd

WHAT CHANGED IN v18 - COLOUR VISION MODES
-----------------------------------------
* COLOUR-BLIND PALETTES: Settings now has a COLOUR VISION section.
  Pick STANDARD, DEUTAN (red-green weak), PROTAN (red-weak) or TRITAN
  (blue-yellow weak) and every gameplay token re-colours itself - the
  four token tints plus the Go/No-Go alert halo all follow your mode.
  A live preview strip shows the exact tokens you'll play with, and a
  hint line explains which vision each mode is tuned for.
* TUNED, NOT GUESSED: every palette was designed against an optical
  simulation of the actual deficiency and verified by the self-test.
  Under the old colours a deutan player saw the RED and YELLOW tokens
  nearly fused (colour difference of just 5 on a 100-point scale - the
  "these look different" line is about 25). The tuned palettes land at
  33-36 on every single token pair, for every mode, against the white
  field included.
* NAMES STAY HONEST: each tint keeps its family - RED still reads red,
  MINT still reads mint - so spoken instructions ("Tap the YELLOW
  ones!", "spot RED and hold back") never contradict what you see.
* ZERO EFFECT ON FAIR PLAY: the palette is pure presentation. Rounds
  are generated and shared as abstract colour names; your phone paints
  them in your mode, your rival's phone paints the same rounds in
  theirs. Seeds, duels, Daily Spot and rival codes are untouched - the
  self-test now forges identical runs under every mode to prove it.
* INSTANT AND PERSISTENT: the mode applies the moment you tap (even
  mid-session, between rounds) and survives restarts like every other
  setting.

WHAT CHANGED IN v17 - RIVAL CODES + LAUNCH SPLASH
-------------------------------------------------
* ASYNC CHALLENGES ARE HERE: friends menu -> CHALLENGE CODE. Every
  player owns a personal challenge code (XXX-XXX-XXX) - a whole
  forged run compressed into 9 characters. Copy it into any chat,
  your friend types it in, and both phones deal the EXACT same
  rounds: same seed, same difficulty anchor, pure fixed curve.
  No servers, no accounts, no internet - the code IS the match.
* PLAY IT FORWARD: play your own challenge once and the results
  screen hands you a one-tap COPY RESULT card ("SpotRush challenge
  - 8/10 correct - 12,340 pts - my run: code ... Beat it!"). Your
  invite text automatically brags the score to beat on your code.
* NEW RUN IN ONE TAP: the NEW key mints a fresh seed as your code;
  any friend who already typed the old one keeps their run - the
  code itself is the whole match, nothing server-side moves.
* TYPE-PROOF CODES: a 6-bit checksum catches mistyped characters,
  near-miss glyphs are forgiven (O reads as 0, I/L as 1), pasted
  text is cleaned and re-grouped while you type, and a bad code
  tells you so instead of dealing garbage. Challenge runs never
  touch the solo records - a replayable code cannot farm the board.
* ENGINE LAUNCH SPLASH: the raw Godot boot flash is gone. The app
  now opens on the game's own lit pad - the exact frame the boot
  screen opens from - so launch reads as one continuous reveal:
  splash, mark blooms, menu rises.

WHAT CHANGED IN v16 - RUN FORGE (the procedural challenge engine)
----------------------------------------------------------------
* EVERY PLAY IS A NEW RUN: each Play click now forges a complete,
  never-before-seen run from fresh entropy - a generated round set,
  not a rotation. No two runs share a schedule, layout or answer key.
* NO MORE HARDCODED ROUNDS: the fixed round order (comparison, then
  estimation, then colour...) is gone. A variety director schedules
  the run with guarantees: no mini-game repeats back to back, every
  type appears in the opening six, the DON'T TAP beat never fires
  twice within three rounds, and the input rhythm never locks into
  three of a kind.
* SMOOTH PROGRESSIVE DIFFICULTY: difficulty is now a continuous
  curve, not four fixed tier tables. Every run starts at your level
  and climbs - with a gentle settle-in on round one and one recovery
  breather just past halfway. In solo play the curve also bends live:
  hot streaks push into harder material faster, misses ease off.
* COMPUTED CHALLENGES: comparison margins scale by Weber fraction
  (the gap is always a fair share of the crowd - 38% down to 7%),
  estimation distractors tighten around the true count as pressure
  rises, search rounds grow confusable distractor sets, and count
  thresholds are constructed around their answer - no constants.
* BALANCED ANSWERS: LEFT/RIGHT, YES/NO and the Go/No-Go outcome are
  drawn from a fairness ledger - never three of a kind in a row and
  the split never drifts past two. No more guessing "it's always
  LEFT".
* NEW RULE FLAVOURS: MORE or FEWER comparisons, AT LEAST or AT MOST
  thresholds, rotating target colours/shapes, and dynamic phrasing -
  instructions are assembled from template pools, so runs stop
  reading like a looped recording.
* GO/NO-GO NO LONGER LEAKS: the confirm key used to tell you
  whether RED was on the field. It doesn't any more - scanning for
  the trigger is the whole challenge now.
* QUALITY GATED: every round is verified before it reaches you -
  token spacing, bounds, option sanity, timing windows - and any
  round that fails is re-forged.
* DAILY SPOT + DUELS: the daily series is still identical worldwide
  (seeded from the date) and duels still deal the same rounds to
  both phones - now through the same forge.
* Run the headless verification suite any time:
  godot --headless -s res://scripts/challenges/ChallengeSystemSelfTest.gd

WHAT CHANGED IN v15 - SCORING 2.0 (the scoring upgrade)
------------------------------------------------------
* HARDER PAYS MORE: base pay now depends on the round's difficulty
  band - WARM-UP 800 / STEADY 1000 / HARD 1250 / EXPERT 1550.
  As your adaptive rating climbs, the stakes climb with it (a
  perfect EXPERT answer at x4 combo is worth 8,800).
* SPEED 2.0: the speed bonus now uses an ease-out curve, so
  mid-window answers earn closer to the max - "answer fast"
  always feels worth it.
* LIGHTNING BONUS: answer with 85%+ of the clock still in hand
  and a flat +150 BOLT lands on top. The feedback badge swaps its
  check mark for a golden bolt when you do.
* ROUND GRADES: every correct answer is graded on how much clock
  you used - S (30% or less), A, B, C. S rounds take over the
  card title ("S RANK!"), and the results screen tallies your
  S-ranks for the run.
* SCORE MATH ON THE CARD: the feedback card now shows the full
  breakdown - e.g. "S - BASE 1,250 + SPEED 318 + BOLT 150  x2" -
  so the scoring system teaches itself.
* NEW BEST ROUND! (solo): beat your all-time best round and the
  card calls it out live. Best round + best streak now persist
  across sessions; the results screen marks new records ("NEW!").
* DIFFICULTY CALLOUTS: GET READY now names the upcoming band
  ("ROUND 3 OF 10 - HARD"), and when the adaptive engine moves
  you up or down a band you HEAR it - two rising notes up, two
  falling notes down.
* DUEL FIX: the combo meter on the joining phone now stays live -
  the breakdown + streak travel with every answer packet, so both
  phones see the same math.
* Big point numbers now use comma formatting everywhere (cards,
  fly-ups, results).

WHAT CHANGED IN v14 - DAILY SPOT (the once-a-day challenge)
-----------------------------------------------------------
* Open the DAILY hub: tap the pill button above the footer
  (the one that used to show your personal best).
* Every day there is ONE Daily Spot: 10 rounds, the exact same
  challenge series for EVERY player (seeded from the calendar
  date) - like the classic once-a-day puzzle games.
* The daily uses the fixed difficulty ramp (1-4 over 10 rounds)
  so the series is identical worldwide. Regular solo play still
  uses your personal adaptive rating.
* DAY STREAK: play daily to grow it (Day 1, 2, 3...).
  STREAK SHIELD: miss exactly one day and the streak survives
  automatically - one shield per week, no setup needed.
  Miss two or more days and the streak restarts.
* Done for today? The hub shows your result and a live countdown
  to the next Spot, and the results-screen key becomes
  "SHARE RESULT" - copies a result card like:
  SpotRush Daily #12 - 9/10 correct - 16,450 pts - x3 max combo
  (v15: the card also brags your S-rank count.)

WHAT CHANGED IN v13 - COMBOS + ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY
---------------------------------------------------
* Combo multiplier tiers: 3 in a row -> x2, 6 -> x3, 10 -> x4.
  The multiplier scales the whole round reward.
  HUD flame chip = combo meter with heat colors + tier jingles.
* Adaptive difficulty: live skill rating (1-4) replaces the fixed
  ramp in solo/duel; persists between sessions.

HOW THE SCORING MATH WORKS NOW
------------------------------
correct answer:
  (band base + eased speed bonus + bolt if 85% clock left) x combo
  WARM-UP 800 | STEADY 1000 | HARD 1250 | EXPERT 1550
  combo tiers: x1 / x2 (3 row) / x3 (6 row) / x4 (10 row)
wrong answer: -150 and the combo resets to x1
perfect restraint (withhold): flat 1,000
grades: S (used 30% of clock or less) / A (55%) / B (80%) / C

HOW THE DAILY FLOW WORKS
------------------------
Menu -> DAILY hub -> PLAY TODAY'S SPOT -> 10 seeded rounds ->
results ("DAILY #N DONE!") -> SHARE RESULT copies the card ->
come back tomorrow after the countdown hits zero.
