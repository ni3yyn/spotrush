## Full description (4000 max)

```
SpotRush is a pocket arcade of split-second judgement. Six mini-games, one rule: see it, decide it, TAP it - before the clock dies.

SOLO RUNS THAT FORGE THEMSELVES
Every run is generated on the spot. No two runs share a schedule, a layout or an answer key. Comparison, counting, colour spotting, shape spotting, and the notorious DON'T TAP rounds where the winning move is not touching the screen at all. Difficulty climbs a smooth curve that adapts to you: hot streaks push you into harder material, misses ease off. Rounds are quality-checked before they ever reach you.

SCORING THAT SHOWS ITS MATH
Speed, difficulty and combo multipliers stack. Answer with most of the clock still in hand and a BOLT bonus lands on top. Perfect answers earn S-ranks, and every card shows the full breakdown - the game teaches you to be faster just by playing.

THE DAILY SPOT
One hand-made-feeling challenge per day, the same ten rounds for every player on Earth. Grow your day streak; miss a single day and the Streak Shield quietly saves it. Share a result card your friends can beat.

DUELS ON ONE WI-FI - NO TYPING
Create a room and your game pops up on your friend's phone. One tap joins. Ten identical rounds, both phones, one winner - everything is seeded and fair. No accounts, no servers, no internet needed beyond your own Wi-Fi or hotspot. (Some restrictive networks hide nearby games; a game code fallback is always there.)

RIVAL CODES: DUELS AT ANY DISTANCE
Every player owns a 9-character challenge code - a whole forged run compressed into text. Send it in any chat. Your friend plays the EXACT same rounds and tries to beat your score. No server in the middle: the code IS the match.

BUILT FOR EVERY PAIR OF EYES
Four colour-vision modes (Deutan, Protan, Tritan) re-tune every token and every halo, tuned against real optical simulations and verified by an automated test suite - gameplay stays perfectly fair between players using different modes.

NO ADS. NO ACCOUNTS. NO NONSENSE.
Nothing to sign, nothing tracking you, nothing selling you anything. All progress lives on your phone. Back button handled, confirmations before anything costs progress, screen stays awake mid-round, real safe-area support on tall phones.

SpotRush is a RUN FORGE game. See it. Decide it. TAP it.
```
(~1,900 chars — room left for future feature lines)
