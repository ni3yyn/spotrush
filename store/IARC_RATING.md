# SpotRush - IARC Content Rating Questionnaire

Play Console → **App content → Content rating → Start questionnaire**.
Category: **Game**. Answers below are truthful against the v21 codebase.

## Questionnaire answers

| Theme | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Violence | Any violent content, even cartoonish? | **No** (players tap coloured dots) |
| Sexual / nudity | Any? | **No** |
| Language | Profanity or crude language? | **No** |
| Substances | Alcohol, tobacco, drugs? | **No** |
| Gambling | Real or simulated gambling, betting, casinos, loot boxes paid for with money? | **No** (no wagers, no loot boxes, no purchases at all) |
| User interaction | Can users share content or communicate with each other (chat, forums, user-generated content visible to others)? | **No** |
| Information sharing | Does the app collect/location/share personal info? | **No** |
| Purchases | Digital purchases in the app? | **No** (the app is free with no IAP) |
| Ads | Contains advertisements? | **No** |
| sharing of location / Device data | — | **No** |

Notes on the two questions reviewers most often get wrong:

1. **"Users can interact"** — local two-player duels connect two nearby phones
   directly; there is no chat, no profiles, no content a player creates that
   another player can see. The discovery beacon contains only a port number.
   Async "rival codes" are machine-generated (seed + checksum) and contain no
   user-authored content. Answer **No**.
2. **"Simulated gambling"** — nothing in SpotRush resembles wagering, slot
   mechanics, card decks or chance-for-reward loops beyond ordinary arcade
   play. Answer **No**.
