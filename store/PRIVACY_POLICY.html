1. The short version

SpotRush does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no third-party services. Nothing you do in the game ever reaches us, because there is no "us" on the network - only your phone.

Everything the game remembers (your settings, records, streaks) lives in private storage on your own device.

Local multiplayer connects two phones directly to each other over your Wi-Fi. That traffic goes phone-to-phone only; the developer never sees it.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect or share any of the following, and we could not even if we wanted to, because the game contains no code that could send us anything:

  • Names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers or any identifier
  • Location data of any kind (the game never queries GPS)
  • Gameplay telemetry, crash reports or analytics
  • Advertising identifiers, or any data for advertising purposes
  • Contacts, photos, microphone or camera content

4. Data stored on your device

The game saves your preferences (sound and music levels, haptics, colour-vision mode), your best runs, combo records and daily-streak progress in the app's private storage on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device except through your device's standard, encrypted Android backup, which is under Google's and your control, not ours;
  • is deleted permanently when you uninstall the app.

The game reads your clipboard only at the moment you tap PASTE to bring in a friend's game code or challenge code, and writes to it only when you tap COPY.

6. Local network play

When you open a duel room, your phone announces the room on your local Wi-Fi with a small discovery signal that contains only a game identifier and a connection port - no name, no account, no device or personal information. When two phones duel, gameplay data (rounds, answers, scores) is exchanged directly between the two devices and is never stored by, or visible to, the developer. No game traffic ever leaves your local network.

7. Children

SpotRush is a colour-and-numbers reaction game with no mature content, and because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either. We recommend it for ages 6 and up purely on reading-comprehension grounds.

8. Third-party services

There are none. No ad networks, no analytics providers, no crash-reporting services, no login providers, no cloud services. The game is built with the open-source Godot Engine.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of SpotRush ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the "last updated" date before that version ships. Play Store listings and the in-game description will always describe the real behaviour.
